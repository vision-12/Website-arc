# Signing in to your school website

## Signing in

1. Go to your school website's address followed by **/login** (for example `www.yourschool.ac.ug/login`).
2. Type the email address your school administrator invited, and your password.
3. Select **Sign in**.

If you see **"Email or password is incorrect"**, check for typing mistakes. After several wrong attempts you will be asked to wait 15 minutes. This protects your account.

## Your first sign-in (invitation)

You will receive an email titled **"You have been invited to manage the school website"**.

1. Select **Accept the invitation and choose a password**. The link works once and expires after 24 hours.
2. Check your name, then choose a password of **at least 12 characters with a letter and a number**. A short sentence you can remember works well, for example _"Lake Victoria sunrise 2027"_.
3. Select **Set up my account**.

## Two-step sign-in (authenticator app)

Some roles can see personal information about pupils and families. For those roles (and for everyone, if your school chooses), signing in needs a code from your phone as well as your password.

**Setting it up (once):**

1. Install an authenticator app on your phone, such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator.
2. When the website shows a square code, open the app, add an account and scan the code. If you can't scan it, choose "enter a setup key" in the app and type the letters shown.
3. Type the 6-digit code from the app and select **Turn on two-step sign-in**.

**Every time you sign in:** after your password, type the 6-digit code the app shows. Codes change every 30 seconds. If a code doesn't work, wait for the next one and check that your phone's clock is set automatically.

**Lost your phone?** Ask your school's Super Administrator to reset your two-step sign-in.

## Forgotten your password

1. On the sign-in page, select **Forgotten your password?**
2. Type your email address and select **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email titled **"Reset your password"** and choose a new password. The link expires after one hour.

You can also change your password while signed in: open **Your account** (top-right menu) → **Email me a password reset link**.

## Signing out

Open the menu with your name (top right) and select **Sign out**. Always sign out on shared computers.
