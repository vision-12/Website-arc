# Website templates

Your school website can use one of four looks, called **templates**. A template decides how pages are laid out and styled. It never changes what your pages say.

## The four templates

| Template                   | Feels                                                                                      | Good for                                                      |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Institutional Classic**  | Formal and structured: a contact bar, your crest and name in the middle, a full-width menu | Schools that want a traditional, trustworthy look             |
| **Modern Academic**        | Spacious and photographic: large pictures, big clear headings, plenty of white space       | Schools with good photographs of their buildings and students |
| **Heritage & Prestige**    | Elegant and historic: serif lettering, framed photographs, your history as a timeline      | Long-established schools with a story to tell                 |
| **Contemporary Community** | Warm and friendly: rounded cards, a floating menu bar, upcoming events first               | Schools with a busy calendar and an active community          |

## What changes when you switch template

- How the header, menus and footer look.
- How each homepage section is laid out, for example the slideshow, news, events and "key numbers".
- How inner pages frame their content.

## What stays exactly the same

- **All your content:** pages, news, events, photographs, staff profiles and documents.
- **Your settings:**
  - your school's name, logo, colours and fonts
  - your menus and footer links
  - the order of your homepage sections, and which ones are hidden
- **Admissions:** applications and enquiries are not touched.

The system checks this. Switching template changes only the template setting, and every switch is recorded in the activity log.

## Previewing a template

From Phase 5, the **Appearance** page has a template picker with a live preview. The preview shows your real homepage in the template you are considering, without saving anything. Preview links last 10 minutes and only work for the person who opened them.

## Good to know

- **Photographs make the biggest difference.** Modern Academic in particular is built around large pictures, so upload a few good, wide photographs before choosing it.
- **The slideshow can be paused.** Visitors can pause it with the button under the pictures. It never moves for visitors who have asked their device to reduce motion.
- **Every template works on phones.** Each one was checked on a 360-pixel-wide phone screen and on a computer, including with a keyboard and a screen reader.
