# Trash

When you delete a page or a file, it goes to **Trash** first. Open **Trash** at the bottom of the menu on the left. You see it if you are allowed to delete something.

- Each item shows when it was moved to Trash, by whom, and **how long it is kept** (30 days).
- **Restore** brings it back exactly as it was. A page that was on the website goes back on the website. If its address was taken in the meantime, it gets a new address ending in a number, and you are told.
- **Delete permanently** removes it for good. Only a Super Administrator can do this, and you must type the item's name to confirm. This can't be undone.

Some things can't be moved to Trash:

- **A page with sub-pages.** Move the sub-pages out, or move them to Trash, first.
- **A built-in page**, such as "About the school".
- **A photo or PDF used on the website.** Replace it, or remove it from those pages first.

Items are not yet removed automatically after 30 days. That arrives with the data-retention settings.
